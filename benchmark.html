ABLATION STUDY

Remove T6 and CRR collapses.

T6 removal collapses CRR from 100% to 37%. TRR stays flat — the hard budget cap still binds — but critical facts are lost. This is why T6 is non-negotiable.

FULL DSPM PIPELINE
100% CRR
TRR: 85.0% · Criticals Retained: 27/27

T6 Shadow Selection inspects candidate pruning sets and guarantees all critical semantic patches are injected before emission.

WITHOUT T6 SHADOW SELECTION
37.04% CRR
TRR: 85.8% · Criticals Retained: 10/27

Without shadow re-injection, standard heuristic pruners drop critical constraints under budget pressure, causing catastrophic constraint loss.

Known Limitations

In the spirit of honest scientific inquiry, the following limitations are explicitly documented and acknowledged in the preprint:

  • • Self-referential consistency evaluation: The same underlying LLM generates turns and acts as an evaluation judge for consistency scoring.
  • • Sample scale: 7 structured synthetic dialogues (280 total turns) tested across 3 adversarial rounds.
  • • Explicitly acknowledged in preprint: Full methodology and raw logs are open for external peer replication.
